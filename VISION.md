# Nutri-Cart — Vision

## Main goal
Nutri-Cart is a **nutrition coach in your pocket**. Every time you scan, search or add something, it nudges you toward healthier choices — with humour, with plain-language facts, and with an honest picture of what is (and isn't) on your cart.

Healthy vs. unhealthy is grounded in **Canada's Food Guide** (Health Canada, https://food-guide.canada.ca/en/): fill half your plate with vegetables and fruit, a quarter with whole grain foods, a quarter with protein foods, make water your drink of choice, and limit highly processed foods.

## Roast mode (kazoo) — all users, all ages, both directions
Roast mode is a universal humour feature (a kazoo plus a short line). It reacts to the **food and the nutrition gap — never the person's body**.

| Situation | What the app does |
|---|---|
| Unhealthy item (outside the Food Guide) | Sad kazoo + roast, e.g. *"Ah ah ah, here — don't eat that. That's not healthy."*, with the plain-language damage on the swap card (calories, sugar, "outside Canada's Food Guide"). |
| Healthy item | Happy kazoo + compliment, e.g. *"Nice — that's what a healthy eater does."* |
| Under-eating / poor nutrition (cart gap) | A roast that names what is missing, e.g. *"This cart has zero protein foods — pencil mode activated ✏️"*, citing the Food Guide plate (half veg & fruit, a quarter protein). |

Rules: roast the food and the nutrition gap, never weight, shape or body. No weight-loss promises anywhere in the app.

## Goals
Stay / maintain, Lean, Strong, and **High-protein / Bulking** (scoring reversed: protein-forward items and gainers score well). Goals adapt *within* Canada's Food Guide. **Keto** is supported as an explicit exception, always labelled *"outside Canada's Food Guide"*, and gets an exact keto buy list. Athletes (e.g. hockey) get protein-forward, performance-minded swaps via Strong / Bulking and the Athletic body-type target.

## Body-type target shapes the swaps
The body-type target (chosen by the user) re-ranks swap suggestions after allergen and diabetes safety filters (safety always wins):

- **Athletic** — protein-forward swaps first.
- **Thin and Average** — balanced default order.
- **Above Average and Obese** — lighter, higher-fibre swaps first.

Each swap card says **"Picked for your <X> target"**.

## The consultant avatar
The cart shows a consultant avatar whose weight stage follows the cart. A **cartoon pimple overlay** scales with how junk-dominated the cart is (share of items outside Canada's Food Guide): under 20% clear skin, 20–49% a few spots, 50–74% moderate, 75% and up many. It is a pure SVG overlay — the avatar art is never changed — and it updates live as the cart changes.

## Not promised
No weight-loss claims, no medical advice, no sodium or saturated-fat scoring until the catalogue carries those values (nothing is guessed).
